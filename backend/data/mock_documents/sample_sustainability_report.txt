Zenlynx Technology — Sample Enterprise Sustainability Report (Mock Data)

Section 1: Emissions Overview
Our organization tracks Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 greenhouse gas emissions
annually. In the most recent reporting period, Scope 1 emissions (direct
emissions from owned sources) totaled 1,250 tCO2e, a 6% reduction from the
prior year, driven by fleet electrification. Scope 2 emissions (purchased
electricity) totaled 3,400 tCO2e, reduced through a shift to renewable energy
contracts covering 40% of grid electricity purchases.

Section 2: Water Stewardship
Water withdrawal across all facilities was 85,000 kiloliters, with 30% sourced
from recycled/treated water. Two facilities in water-stressed regions have
water-efficiency retrofits planned for the next fiscal year.

Section 3: Waste & Circular Economy
Total waste generated was 2,100 tonnes, of which 68% was diverted from
landfill through recycling and composting programs. A packaging redesign
initiative aims to reduce single-use plastic packaging by 25% within two years.

Section 4: Biodiversity
An environmental impact assessment was conducted for the proposed expansion
site, identifying two protected species habitats within a 5km radius. Mitigation
measures include a buffer zone and a biodiversity offset program.

Section 5: Regulatory Compliance
The organization is preparing for CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting
Directive) compliance ahead of the mandatory reporting deadline, and has
aligned its disclosure framework with BRSR Core requirements for the Indian
market, covering the nine ESG attributes required under SEBI's framework.
